Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

Lamoon is a menu-bar screen-dimming utility for macOS. It runs entirely on your Mac and does not send personal data to the developer or any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising, or telemetry SDKs.

When you enable Solar Scheduling, Lamoon requests your approximate location through macOS Location Services. The coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and stored locally on your Mac for that feature only. This location data is not transmitted anywhere.

What Lamoon does locally

Screen dimming

Lamoon adjusts screen brightness using either an overlay film window or your display's gamma table. All processing happens on your Mac.

Location (optional)

Only if you enable "night scheduling by sunrise/sunset", Lamoon requests your approximate location through macOS Location Services. The request happens when you turn on this feature. Once granted, coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and stored locally on your Mac. If permission is denied or location is unavailable, manual scheduling is used instead. Lamoon does not transmit coordinates to the developer or any third party.

Preferences

Your settings (dim level, warmth, white point, tone, language, hotkeys, schedule) are stored locally in macOS UserDefaults within Lamoon's sandboxed container.

No telemetry

Lamoon contains no analytics, crash reporting, or network upload code.

macOS Location Services

When you enable solar scheduling, macOS Location Services provides your approximate position. This is governed by Apple's own terms and your system settings. Lamoon itself does not store or transmit raw location data beyond the rounded value described above.

Data retention

Preferences and location data stay on your Mac. Lamoon stores them in macOS UserDefaults within its sandboxed container. Deleting the app does not automatically remove this container; to fully remove stored preferences and location data, delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.maimajor.lamoon/ manually after uninstalling.

Third parties

Lamoon does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising networks.

Technical Details (for verification)

App Sandbox: enabled. Entitlements: app-sandbox + personal-information.location (used only for solar scheduling). Privacy manifest: NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults (CA92.1), no collected data types, no tracking. No URLSession or network code in source. Frameworks used: Cocoa, SwiftUI, ServiceManagement, CoreLocation, os.
